Certificate of completion for the "L'Atelier RGPD" MOOC, the official online training provided by the CNIL (French Data Protection Authority).

This certification validates the acquisition of essential knowledge regarding the General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR) and privacy protection, including:
* Understanding the fundamental principles of personal data protection.
* Identifying the roles and obligations of professionals (data controllers and processors).
* Respecting and facilitating the privacy rights of data subjects.
* Integrating GDPR compliance into IT projects from the design phase (Privacy by Design).
