Certificate of completion for the "Introduction to Cybersecurity" course, issued by Cisco through the Skills for All learning platform.

This introductory certification validates the understanding of basic IT security concepts, including:
* The importance of cybersecurity and the global impact of cyberattacks.
* Identifying common cyber threats, vulnerabilities, and types of malicious software (malware).
* Understanding the methods used to protect against and mitigate risks to networks and data.
* Exploring career opportunities in the cybersecurity field.
