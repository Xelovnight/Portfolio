The MOOC GdP is a widely recognized online training program that introduces the fundamentals of project management. 

It covers key stages such as project scoping, planning, risk management, the use of dedicated tools, tracking, and project closure. The learning path also includes specialization modules (agile methods, functional analysis, team management, ROI calculation) that allow for deepening specific skills. This certification validates a comprehensive approach combining theory, practical exercises, and concrete case studies.
