Certificate of completion for the SecNumacadémie MOOC, provided by ANSSI (French National Cybersecurity Agency).

This certification validates the understanding of fundamental concepts in information systems security, including:
* Authentication and password security.
* Internet security and safe browsing habits.
* Work environment security (desktop and remote work).
* Awareness of current cyber threats and the application of IT security best practices.
