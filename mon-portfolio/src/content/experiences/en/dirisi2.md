A 5-week internship completed during my second year of the BTS SIO (SLAM) degree, focusing on the integration and deployment of Artificial Intelligence solutions.

**Context and Proof of Concept (PoC)**
The primary mission was to research and deploy a local, offline, open-source, and free alternative to document-analysis AIs. The goal was to provide a secure tool for the Ministry of Armed Forces to facilitate smart note-taking and the analysis of complex documents.

**Environment Setup and Deployment**
* Architectural design and full environment containerization using **Docker**.
* Simultaneous deployment of the **SurrealDB** newSQL database, the **Ollama** AI engine, and the **OpenNotebook AI** platform.

**AI Configuration and Testing**
* Importing and configuring specialized AI models running 100% locally: **LLaMA 3.2** for natural language processing and **mxbai-embed-large** for vector embedding.
* Application configuration to handle isolated environment limitations (e.g., bypassing unsupported audio transcription models).
* Comprehensive functional testing: workspace creation, multi-format source document injection, and validation of AI-assisted tasks.
